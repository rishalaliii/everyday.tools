FAQ

Are spaces counted?

Both totals are shown: characters with spaces and without.

How are sentences counted?

Text is split at . ! or ? followed by a space or the end. Abbreviations such as "e.g." can add extra sentences.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser.

FAQ

Why does it sometimes fail?

YouTube does not allow free key-less browser access, so this tool uses free community servers (Piped/Invidious). They can be slow, rate-limited or offline. Try again later.

Can it read private playlists?

No. The playlist must be public or unlisted.

Why are some videos not counted?

Deleted, private, restricted and live videos have no length.

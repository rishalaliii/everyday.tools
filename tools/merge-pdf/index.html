Merge PDF

Combine several PDF files into one, in the order you choose. Processed in your browser; your files are not uploaded.

or drop PDFs here. Add more at any time. Up to 50 files, 100 MB each. Nothing is uploaded.

    FAQ

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. Merging runs entirely in your browser; the files never leave your device.

    Can I change the order?

    Yes. Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons next to each file, or ✕ to remove one.

    Why was my file refused?

    Password-protected or damaged PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the protection in a PDF viewer first.

    What is not kept?

    Bookmarks, form fields and document-level tags may not be carried over; page content, images, text and links are.

PDF to Word (DOCX)

Convert selectable PDF text into an editable Word document. The output preserves text and page breaks, but complex layouts, tables, columns and scanned pages may not match the original. Scanned PDFs require OCR, which is not included.

Your PDF is processed in this browser. The DOCX generator is loaded from a public CDN when needed.

File to PDF Converter

Convert common images and text-based files into PDF documents. Files are processed locally; no upload to EverydayTools.

Images, text files and DOCX Word documents. Unsupported formats show a clear error.

FAQ

Which formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, TXT, CSV, Markdown, HTML, JSON, XML, LOG, SRT and DOCX Word documents. Older DOC files, spreadsheets, presentations and arbitrary binary formats require a dedicated converter or export from their original app.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens in your browser.

Can I convert multiple files?

Yes. Supported files are combined into one PDF in the order selected.

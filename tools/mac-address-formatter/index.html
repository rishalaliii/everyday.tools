FAQ

Which formats are accepted?

AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF, AA-BB-CC-DD-EE-FF, AABB.CCDD.EEFF and 12 plain hex digits.

Does it look up the manufacturer?

No. It shows the first three bytes (OUI) only; vendor lookup needs an external database.

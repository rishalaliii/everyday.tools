    FAQ

    Which images work?

    JPG and PNG are embedded directly. WebP, GIF (first frame) and BMP are converted in your browser first. HEIC and SVG are not supported.

    Will my photos be rotated correctly?

    Yes. Photos with a camera rotation flag are straightened before they go into the PDF.

    Is image quality reduced?

    JPG and PNG files are embedded without recompression, so quality is kept (rotated JPEGs are re-saved at high quality).

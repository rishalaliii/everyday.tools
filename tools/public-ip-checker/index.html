Public IP Checker

See the public IP address websites see for you.

This tool contacts an external service (api.ipify.org) to learn your public IP address. That service sees your IP address like any website you visit. Nothing else is sent.

FAQ

Why does it differ from my router IP?

Your router has a private address on your network; the public address belongs to your ISP, VPN or proxy.

It fails or times out.

The service may be blocked by an ad blocker, firewall or VPN, or you may be offline.

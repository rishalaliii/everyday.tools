FAQ

Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser, so the file never leaves your device.

Why is my PNG not smaller?

PNG is lossless and ignores the quality slider. Choose JPG or WebP, or set a maximum width.

Is transparency kept?

In PNG and WebP, yes. JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas become white.

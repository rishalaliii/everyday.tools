    FAQ

    How do ranges work?

    Each comma-separated item becomes its own PDF: "1-3, 4, 5-" gives three files. Use a number for one page, a-b for a range, a- for "to the end".

    Can I download everything at once?

    Yes. After splitting you get each file as a link plus a ZIP of all parts.

    What is not kept?

    Bookmarks, form fields and document-level tags may not be carried over; page content, images, text and links are.

FAQ

What is a wildcard mask?

The inverse of a subnet mask, used in Cisco ACLs and OSPF network statements (0.0.0.255 for a /24).

Why is my mask rejected?

Subnet masks must be a run of 1 bits followed by 0 bits, such as 255.255.248.0.

Privacy Policy

Your inputs. Text, numbers and files you use in the tools are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. We do not have user accounts or a database.

Stored in your browser. The site saves your theme choice, recently used tools and, for the YouTube calculator, your progress, using your browser's local storage. You can clear this in your browser settings.

YouTube import. If you use the optional playlist import, your browser sends the playlist ID and your own API key directly to Google's YouTube Data API. Google's privacy policy applies to that request. We never receive your key.

Hosting and advertising. The site is hosted on a static hosting service that may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested). If advertising such as Google AdSense is added in future, ad providers may use cookies; this policy will be updated before that happens and, where required, consent will be requested.

Children. The site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. We may update this policy; the current version is always on this page.

Questions: see Contact.
